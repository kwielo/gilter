Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Overview

Gilter ("the App") is a Gmail filter editor operated by wielo.co ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle your data when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. Gilter is a client-side application — all processing happens entirely in your browser.

  • Gmail filter data — When you connect your Google account, filter data is fetched directly from Google's servers to your browser. It is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Google account email — Displayed in the UI for your reference only. Not stored or transmitted.
  • Local storage — Filter data may be saved in your browser's localStorage for session persistence. This data stays on your device.

3. Google API Usage

Gilter uses the Google Gmail API with the following OAuth scopes:

  • gmail.settings.basic — To read, create, and delete Gmail filters.
  • gmail.labels — To read your Gmail label names for filter display.

Gilter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage

All data remains in your browser. We do not operate any backend servers that receive or store your data. The App is hosted as a static website on Cloudflare Workers.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google APIs — Used for Gmail filter and label access. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — Hosts the static application files. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies

Gilter does not use cookies. Browser localStorage is used solely for persisting your filter editing session.

7. Children's Privacy

Gilter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@wielo.co
